About Michael Mando

Michael Mando is a Canadian actor, writer, and director best known for his intense and memorable performances in television and video games. He gained international acclaim for portraying the iconic villain Vaas Montenegro in the video game Far Cry 3, where his likeness and voice acting set a new standard for gaming antagonists. He further solidified his status in Hollywood by playing Nacho Varga in the critically acclaimed crime drama series Better Call Saul.

Important Career Milestones

  1. 1981: Born in Quebec City, Canada, and raised by a single father.
  2. 2010: Founded his own production company, Redboy Productions, and starred in the award-winning indie film Conditional.
  3. 2012: Achieved worldwide fame as the voice and physical model for Vaas Montenegro in Ubisoft's hit game Far Cry 3.
  4. 2015–2022: Starred as Ignacio "Nacho" Varga in all six seasons of the Breaking Bad prequel series Better Call Saul.
  5. 2017: Appeared as Mac Gargan in Marvel Studios' superhero film Spider-Man: Homecoming.

Key Roles & Contributions

  • Vaas Montenegro in Far Cry 3 (and reprised the role in Far Cry 6)
  • Nacho Varga in the Emmy-nominated series Better Call Saul
  • Vic Schmidt in the sci-fi thriller series Orphan Black
  • Mac Gargan in Spider-Man: Homecoming
